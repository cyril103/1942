PACIFIC STRIKE ULTIMATE — CAMPAIGN 1942
Version 2.3.0 — French / English

GETTING STARTED
Run PacificStrike.exe. No Godot installation is required.
Windows x64, OpenGL 3.3. Keyboard and controller supported.

LANGUAGE
The default language is French. Use Français / English on the title screen,
or open Options > Langue / Language and select English.
You can also change language from the pause menu. The change takes effect
immediately and is remembered next time you play. Existing saves retain their
progress, controls and French language until you choose otherwise.

FLIGHT CONTROLS
Arrow keys, WASD or ZQSD: move. Hold Space: fire. Hold Shift: precision flight.
X: bomb. C: aircraft ability when the special gauge reaches 100%.
Escape: pause or go back. Escape on the title screen exits the game.
Controller: left stick or D-pad to move, A to fire, B for a bomb, X for your
aircraft ability, LB for precision flight and Start to pause.
Controls can be remapped in Options. In-game hints reflect your bindings.
Optional auto-fire is available in the Controls menu and is off by default.

READING THE BATTLE
Your shots are cyan and white; enemy projectiles are orange and red.
Precision flight slows movement and reveals the vulnerable centre of your plane.
Boss attack warnings show the incoming pattern and an evasion tip. Watch the
charge, then dodge the actual bullets. The two golden rings mark weak points;
destroying them reduces the boss's firepower. Bosses have three phases.
Keep bombs for moments when you are about to be surrounded.

POWER-UPS AND SCORING
Destroy all five aircraft in a red formation to release a POW pickup:
cyan fan = spread fire; violet beam = laser; green plane with + = extra life.
Each pickup replaces your current weapon. The extra life is granted immediately
and restores standard fire; a later pickup does not remove that life.
Hold your fire button to sustain the laser, just like standard shots.

Destroy another enemy within 4.5 seconds to maintain your chain. Each group of
eight kills increases the multiplier, up to x5. Taking a hit breaks the chain.
A complete fighter wave awards 100 points per aircraft (800 for eight fighters).
One escaping aircraft cancels the perfect-wave bonus. Clearing a red formation
awards 1,000 points as well as a POW. A secondary objective awards 2,500 once.
Grazing an enemy bullet safely awards 25 points and up to 0.6 special charge.
Each bullet can reward you once. Grazes do not extend your kill chain.

ARMOR, LIVES AND CHECKPOINTS
Armor is the number of hits you can take before losing an aircraft and a life.
If lives remain, you return after 1.5 seconds with full armor and four seconds
of protection. Your score and progress within the current mission are retained.
At zero lives, choose whether to retry the mission checkpoint or return home.

Campaign score, remaining lives and equipped weapon carry between missions.
Progress saves automatically between missions, rather than during flight.
Retrying restores the starting checkpoint, preventing duplicated points.
Unlocked missions remain replayable. New Campaign asks for confirmation and
resets progression while retaining your settings and high score.

CAMPAIGN AND HANGAR
The campaign spans 32 missions across eight sectors. Read the briefing for your
primary objective, secondary objective and gold-medal conditions.
Campaign medals and first victories earn parts for permanent upgrades.

Vanguard: highest sustained fire rate, with balanced speed and armor.
Interceptor: P-38 Lightning, 16.7% faster with 10% lower standard DPS than Vanguard;
the same armor and hitbox. Its ability boosts speed and damage for four seconds.
Bulwark: F4U Corsair, one extra armor point and bomb; 13.3% slower with 18.2% lower
standard DPS. Its ability grants protection and damage for 3.5 seconds with an
area strike. Vanguard's ability boosts its fire rate for five seconds.
Abilities preserve your POW weapon. Special charge pauses while an ability runs.

Upgrade ranks I / II / III cost 2 / 4 / 6 parts and unlock at missions 01 / 09 / 21.
Ten optional modules cost six parts each. The first slot unlocks at mission 05;
the second at mission 13. Equip one module per family: Aim, Airframe or Systems.
Buying a module does not equip it. Equipping and removing are free.
The hangar shows actual equipped stats before POW and ability bonuses.
Inspect Aircraft opens a 3D view: drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, or use the
view buttons with your keyboard/controller. Assign Aircraft confirms selection.

MEDALS AND RANKS
Bronze: victory. Silver: victory without losing a life.
Gold: victory, no lost lives, at most two hits and the primary objective complete.
S rank: victory, no lost lives, at most one hit, the secondary objective complete
and a best chain of at least 16. Medals and ranks measure different achievements.
The briefing and results screens provide the full scoring guide.

GROUND RAIDS
Missions 03, 07, 11, 15, 19, 23, 27 and 31 approach over water, descend over a base,
then climb for extraction and return to the carrier. Altitude changes are
automatic; you retain flight control. Your weapons can damage installations.
Complete both the required target quota and every marked priority target.
A priority target counts toward the quota. If it escapes intact, retry the raid.
Destroying a radar jams its linked AA network for six seconds. Only the first
raid uses a network covering the whole base. Fuel tanks damage nearby defenses
when they explode. Mobile AA stops before firing; move after its warning.
Aircraft defend the sea approach and extraction; AA takes over at low altitude.
An incomplete raid returns you to the deck, with the missing conditions shown
in the debriefing. For a quick try, choose Arcade / Score Attack, mission 03.

OTHER MODES
Arcade: all 32 missions, Pilot difficulty, no upgrades, three lives, starting
score zero. Local records are separate for each mission and aircraft.
Boss Training: direct access to boss fights with spread fire.
Boss Rush: eight sector bosses, three shared lives, Pilot difficulty and fixed
equipment. Armor and bombs replenish between duels; score and lives carry over.
A defeat ends the run. Restart begins again at the first boss. Records are
awarded only after all eight victories and are tracked per aircraft.
These modes do not change campaign progress, parts, medals or campaign score.

COMFORT AND DISPLAY
Options include master/music/effects volume, fullscreen, camera shake, reduced
flashes, VSync and an FPS display. Graphics offers Performance, Balanced and
Quality presets. Performance disables dynamic shadows and optional debris.
Short retry intros are optional: once you have completed a takeoff in a mission
and mode this session, retries can use a 1.6-second stabilization. First visits
retain the full takeoff; the normal carrier landing remains unchanged.

The animated ocean, coastal foam and clouds are decorative. Clouds do not affect
weapons, collisions or difficulty. Combat music by Juhani Junkala is credited
in game. Component licenses and credits are included in the Licences folder.
